For Facility Administrators

We Bring the Clinic to You

Fenkell Rx Pharmacy administers on-site vaccination clinics at nursing homes, assisted living facilities, businesses, schools, churches, and community organizations across the Detroit area. Our pharmacists handle all the logistics — you just need a space and at least 5 participants. We bill each person's insurance directly, so there is no administrative burden on your organization for insured participants.

No upfront cost for insured participants We bill Medicare Part B, Medicaid, and most private insurance plans directly. For most participants, there is no cost to the facility and no out-of-pocket cost to the patient.
What's Included

What Fenkell Rx Provides

Flu Vaccine (Influenza)

Seasonal influenza vaccines for all eligible participants. High-dose formulations available for adults 65 and older upon request.

COVID Vaccine

Current-season COVID vaccines for eligible participants. We stay current with CDC recommendations and can advise on which formulation is appropriate.

Insurance Billing

We handle all billing directly with Medicare, Medicaid, and private insurance. No paperwork for the facility. Consent forms handled on-site.

Licensed Pharmacists On-Site

All vaccines are administered by licensed pharmacists. We bring all necessary supplies and handle post-vaccination observation requirements.

How It Works

From Inquiry to Clinic Day

1
Submit an inquiry Use the form below or call (313) 519-5700. Tell us your facility name, estimated number of participants, which vaccines you need, and your preferred timeframe.
2
We confirm details and schedule a date A member of our team will contact you to confirm the date, time, and any logistics. We recommend scheduling at least 1 to 2 weeks in advance; fall flu season dates book earlier.
3
Participants complete consent forms We'll provide consent and insurance collection forms in advance so participants can have them ready. Our pharmacists can also assist on clinic day.
4
We administer vaccines on-site Our licensed pharmacists arrive with all supplies. We administer vaccines, handle all billing, and provide each participant with a vaccination record.
