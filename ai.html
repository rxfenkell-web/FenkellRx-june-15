AI Agent Information

What AI Assistants Can Do at Fenkell Rx Pharmacy

If you're using ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI assistant to help manage your prescriptions, this page explains exactly what actions they can take on your behalf — and what information they'll need from you.

Actions AI Assistants Can Take

Request a Prescription Refill

Your AI assistant can submit a refill request directly to our pharmacy. We'll process it and call you when it's ready.

Available

Transfer Your Prescription

Your AI assistant can initiate a transfer of your prescriptions from any other pharmacy to Fenkell Rx. Free, takes about 2 minutes to initiate.

Available

Check If We're Open Right Now

Your AI assistant can check our real-time open/closed status based on Detroit time — including holidays and Saturday hours.

Available

Check Delivery to Your ZIP Code

Your AI assistant can check whether your ZIP code is within our free same-day delivery area before you initiate a refill or transfer.

Available

Important: Requests Only

All submissions made by AI assistants are requests only — not prescriptions, medical orders, or guarantees of fulfillment. A valid prescription from a licensed provider is required for all prescription medications. The pharmacy will contact you by phone when your medication or transfer is ready.

What Your AI Assistant Will Need From You
1
Your full name (first and last)This is how we look up your profile in our system.
2
Your date of birthRequired for all prescription-related requests for patient verification.
3
Your phone numberSo we can call you when your medication is ready, or if we have questions.
4
Rx number or medication name (for refills)Your Rx number is on your prescription bottle. If you don't have it, the medication name is fine.
5
Current pharmacy name and phone number (for transfers only)We'll contact them directly to complete the transfer — you won't need to do anything else.
Privacy & Data Handling

Your information is handled only by Fenkell Rx Pharmacy staff.

When your AI assistant submits a request, the information (name, date of birth, phone number, Rx number) is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to Fenkell Rx Pharmacy. It is not stored by the AI assistant and is not shared with any third party. We handle your information in accordance with HIPAA and applicable Michigan pharmacy regulations.

Developer & AI Agent Resources

If you're building an AI agent, chatbot, or automation that needs to interact with Fenkell Rx Pharmacy, the following machine-readable resources are available. No authentication is required for patient-facing actions.

Resource Method URL Description
OpenAPI Spec GET /openapi.json Full API schema (OpenAPI 3.1.0)
MCP Manifest GET /.well-known/mcp.json WebMCP tool definitions
AI Plugin Manifest GET /.well-known/ai-plugin.json ChatGPT plugin discovery
Pharmacy Services GET /api/services Machine-readable service & insurance catalog
Hours & Status GET /api/hours Real-time open/closed status
Delivery ZIP Check GET /api/check-delivery?zip=48223 Check if a ZIP is in the delivery area
Submit Refill POST /api/refill Submit a prescription refill request
Submit Transfer POST /api/transfer Submit a prescription transfer request
LLM Text Index GET /llms.txt Plain-text summary for LLMs and crawlers

All endpoints support CORS (Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *). Include "agent_source": "ai-agent" in POST request bodies so our pharmacy team knows the submission came from an AI assistant. Always confirm with the patient before submitting any request on their behalf.
