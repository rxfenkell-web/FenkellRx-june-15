For Caregivers

Managing a Loved One's Medications Shouldn't Be a Second Job

Whether you're an adult child coordinating care from across town, a family member living with an elderly parent, or a professional home health aide, managing multiple prescriptions for a homebound patient is a real challenge. Fenkell Rx Pharmacy makes it easier by delivering prescriptions directly to the patient's door — for free — and letting caregivers call in, coordinate refills, and manage medications on behalf of their patients.

Fenkell Rx delivers free to elderly and homebound patients in Detroit No delivery fee. No minimum order. No subscription required. Call (313) 519-5700 to set up delivery for your patient today.
What We Offer

Services Designed for Elderly Patients and Their Caregivers

Free Same-Day Delivery

We deliver prescriptions to patients' homes at no charge. Covers Northwest Detroit, Redford Township, Southfield, and surrounding communities.

Medication Synchronization

We sync all of your patient's prescriptions to refill on the same day each month — one delivery, one call, no missed doses.

Blister Packaging

Medications organized into blister (bubble) packs by day and time. Reduces missed doses and makes caregiver check-ins simpler.

Caregiver Coordination

Caregivers can call on behalf of patients to request refills, arrange delivery, and ask medication questions. No need for the patient to call.

Getting Started

How to Set Up Delivery for Your Patient

1
Call us or submit a transfer request online Call (313) 519-5700 and let us know you're a caregiver setting up delivery for a patient, or use our online transfer form to move your patient's prescriptions to Fenkell Rx. No phone call needed for the transfer itself.
2
We handle the prescription transfer We contact the patient's current pharmacy to transfer all their prescriptions. Caregivers don't need to coordinate between pharmacies — we do that for you.
3
Choose delivery or blister packaging Tell us if you'd like medications delivered directly or organized into blister packs. We can also set up medication synchronization so all refills arrive on the same schedule.
4
We deliver — free, same day When prescriptions are ready, we deliver to the patient's home at no cost. Caregivers receive updates and can call anytime to check status or request early refills.
