Simple Process

How Free Delivery Works

Getting your medications delivered is straightforward. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you sign up to when your prescription arrives at your door.

1
Set Up Your Profile

Call us at (313) 519-5700 or transfer your prescription online. We get your insurance, delivery address, and any special instructions on file — takes just a few minutes.

2
We Fill & Review

Our licensed pharmacists verify your prescription, check for interactions with your other medications, and fill your order accurately. Every prescription gets a full clinical review before it leaves our hands.

3
Delivered to Your Door

Your medications arrive in a sealed, discreet package — clearly labeled with your name and dosing instructions. Most deliveries are same-day or next-day. A signature may be required for controlled substances.
