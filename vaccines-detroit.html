Vaccines We Offer

No scheduling required. Our certified pharmacist immunizers are on-site Monday–Friday 9:30 AM–6:00 PM and Saturday 9:30 AM–3:00 PM at 18360 Fenkell Ave, Detroit, MI 48223.

Flu Shot (Influenza)

Annual influenza vaccine for all ages. The single most important thing you can do each fall to protect yourself and the people around you.

All ages · Annual

COVID-19 Booster

Updated formula targeting current strains. Recommended yearly alongside the flu shot for most adults and all high-risk individuals.

All ages · Updated formula

Shingrix (Shingles)

Two-dose series for adults 50 and older. Over 90% effective — far stronger than the older Zostavax. Covered by Medicare Part D.

Ages 50+ · 2-dose series

RSV Vaccine

Single-dose protection against respiratory syncytial virus for adults 60 and older. Covered by Medicare Part B.

Ages 60+ · Single dose

Pneumococcal (Pneumonia)

Protects against bacterial pneumonia. Recommended for adults 65+ and younger adults with certain health conditions.

Ages 65+ or high-risk

Tdap / Td

Tetanus, diphtheria, and pertussis (whooping cough). Adults need a Td booster every 10 years; Tdap once as an adult booster.

All ages · Every 10 yrs

Hepatitis A & B

Recommended for travel, occupational exposure, or personal protection. Available individually or as a combination series.

Travel · Occupational

Travel Vaccines

Typhoid, Yellow Fever, Hepatitis A, and more. Tell us your destination — our pharmacist will advise on the right combination.

Destination-specific
