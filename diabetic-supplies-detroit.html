What We Carry

Everything you need to manage your diabetes

We stock a full range of diabetic testing supplies and work directly with your insurance so you're never paying more than you should. Most supplies are covered with a valid prescription.

Blood Glucose Meters

AccuChek, OneTouch, and Contour meters available. We help you pick the right meter for your lifestyle and verify your insurance before you leave the counter.

Most Insurance Covered

Test Strips

Compatible strips for all major meter brands. Covered by Medicare, Medicaid, and most private insurance plans with a valid prescription — we verify your coverage before dispensing.

Most Insurance Covered

Lancets & Lancing Devices

Fine-gauge lancets and adjustable lancing devices for nearly painless testing. Covered by Medicare, Medicaid, and most private insurance plans alongside your test strips.

Most Insurance Covered

Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGM)

Dexcom G7 sensors and transmitters, FreeStyle Libre 2 and Libre 3 sensors. CGMs are covered by most insurance plans and Medicare with a valid prescription from your doctor.

Most Insurance Covered

Insulin Syringes & Pen Needles

Full range of insulin syringes and pen needle sizes for all major insulin pens. Our pharmacist advises on the right gauge and length for your injection site and comfort.

Most Insurance Covered

Alcohol Prep Pads

Sterile alcohol swabs for skin prep before injections and fingerstick testing. Most insurance plans — including Medicare and Medicaid — cover alcohol pads for patients using insulin.
